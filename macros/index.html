Macro Guide

AION 2 Global · how the in-game skill macro works, how to set it up, and what each class puts in it

In short

updated 18 Oct 2026

Bind the Macro key, put the one or two hotkeys you spam into the macro, and hold the Macro key together with left click. The macro keeps your main skills firing; left click weaves basic attacks between them, and each basic attack cuts off the skill's recovery animation so the next skill starts sooner. Buffs, movement and charge skills stay out of it.

The macro, while you hold its key

Step 1a hotkey→ 10 ms → Step 2another hotkey→ 10 ms → Step 1 again… It keeps retrying each step in turn until you let go. A step fires whatever on that hotkey is ready.
  • A step is a hotkeyEach step points at one of your quick-slot hotkeys, not at a skill, so it fires every skill stacked on that key: the bottom one when it is ready, the others in between.
  • Priorities, not a scriptThe macro moves on while it retries, so the order is not guaranteed. A step listed twice comes up twice as often.
  • Your presses winPress any skill while holding the Macro key and it fires straight away. You never need to let go.
  • One per presetEach skill preset keeps its own macro, and switching preset stops the one that is running.
  • Built for cancellingNC added it to make basic-attack cancels easier: it handles the skills while you hold left click for the basic attacks.

Set it up in game

about five minutes, once per preset
  1. 1Bind the Macro keySettings → Key Settings → General → Gameplay → Macro. It is unbound by default. A mouse side button is the usual choice, but in the AION 1 control mode mouse buttons do not work as the Macro key, so use a keyboard key there.
  2. 2Stack your hotkeysOpen the Skills window (K) and use its quick-slot grid. Put the skills you spam together on one hotkey, up to four, with your main damage skill at the bottom.
  3. 3Build the macroSkills window → Macro (top right) → Add Macro, then pick a hotkey for the step. A new step starts as the basic attack: change it, or you get extra basic attacks and fewer skills.
  4. 4Set the delay10 ms on each step. On a slower PC, try 15–20 and compare on a training dummy.
  5. 5Leave Skill Queue onSettings → Combat → Controls → General Control Mode Settings → Skill Queue. It is on by default and gave more damage in testing.
  6. 6Repeat on every preset you useFor example a boss preset and a trash preset, each with its own skill levels, Stigmas and macro. You cannot switch presets while a Stigma is cooling down.
  7. 7Test itOn a training dummy, hold left click and the Macro key together. Each successful cancel shows a black silhouette on your character.

What goes in it, and what you press yourself

WhereWhatWhy
In the macroThe one or two hotkeys you spam non-stopEvery extra step costs hits.
Held with itLeft click, the basic attackAs a macro step it fires once a cycle; held, it cancels after every skill.
By handBuffs, Stigma buffs, movement and burst skillsIn the macro a buff fires whenever it is ready, trash included, and lowers your hit count.
Its own held keyCharge skills: Hellfire, Punishment, Deadshot and the likeThe macro fires them at the first charge stage; holding their own key charges them fully.
  • Do not stack two main damage skills, or two skills with the same cooldown, on one hotkey. They block each other.
  • A buff step before your main step can cost hits. Keep buffs out, or put them in a second line at most.
  • A held skill key repeats, but slower than a macro step. There is no hold-to-repeat setting. If a held key stops firing in a long fight, let go and hold it again.
  • Farming: you can add the basic attack as a step so one held key does everything.

Basic-attack cancel

why you hold left click

Every skill ends in a recovery animation. A basic attack right after the skill cuts that animation off, so the next skill starts sooner and more skills fit into a fight.

By hand: press the skill, then left click within about 0.05–0.1 s, and treat the pair as one input. Mashing both buttons gets about half your basic attacks eaten. Every skill has a minimum cancel time, and high ping eats into the window. Holding left click with the Macro key does all of this for you.

If it is not working

What you seeLikely causeFix
The Macro key does nothingA mouse button bound in the AION 1 control modeBind a keyboard key, or switch control mode.
The macro stopped after a preset swapEach preset has its own macro; swapping stops itSet it up on every preset and hold the key again.
A buff went off on trashIt is in the macroTake it out and press it yourself.
Low hit count on the dummyToo many steps, buffs inside, or a delay that does not suit your PCCut to one or two steps, move buffs out, try 15–20 ms.
A charge skill hits weaklyThe macro fires it at the first stagePut it on its own key and hold it.
Extra basic attacks, fewer skillsA step still set to the basic attack (the default)Change or delete that step.
Basic attacks stop while holdingAlt held, or Alt combinations bound on other keysDo not bind skills to Alt combinations.
Skills fire out of orderExpected: the list is a set of prioritiesNothing to fix.
Pressed buffs lag, or basic attacks get eatenSkill QueueMost setups run it on; test both ways.
No black silhouettesLeft click is not held with the Macro keyHold both.

In-game macro or mouse software?

Many guides from the Korean and Taiwanese release add mouse-software sequences or rapid-fire on top of the macro. Measured against those setups, the in-game macro alone came out between about 5% below and 7% above, and a repeated test found no difference. The planners on this site use only the in-game macro. Anything outside the game is covered by NC's rules: read the AION 2 Global operation policy before you use it.
